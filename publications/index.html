  4. Drones
    Uncertainty-Calibrated UAV Trajectory Prediction for Beam Management in UAV-Assisted ISAC Scenarios
    Qing Cheng, Wenwen Wu, and Ziwei Zhao
    Drones, 2026
    Abs HTML

    A multimodal trajectory-prediction framework fusing visual context (ResNet-18) and motion history (LSTM) via cross-attention and gated fusion to output calibrated probabilistic forecasts. Split conformal calibration converts raw Gaussian uncertainty into statistically reliable risk bounds, improving Coverage@95 by 7 percentage points.

2025

  1. PR (under review)
    Information Density Imbalance in Visual Object Detection
    Ziwei Zhao, Yanxi Lu, Yuwei Hu, and 8 more authors
    Under review at Pattern Recognition, 2025
    Abs PDF

    We define categorized information density as a per-class difficulty signal beyond instance frequency. Its Pearson correlation with AP reaches about -0.72 on Pascal VOC, roughly four times stronger than the instance-count baseline, exposing a long-overlooked bias in long-tailed detector training. A local-to-global covariance merging scheme keeps global information density exact during training and reduces per-class AP variance by up to 45.89% over EFL on LVIS v1.0.

  2. arXiv
    Geometric Prior-Guided Federated Prompt Calibration
    Fei Luo, Ziwei Zhao, Mingxuan Wang, and 5 more authors
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2512.07208, 2025
    Abs arXiv

    GGTPC addresses local training bias in federated prompt learning: the server reconstructs global geometric priors from per-class statistics without exchanging raw features. As a plug-and-play module across 7 mainstream FL algorithms, it yields +9.17 pp over FedAvg on CIFAR-100 at beta=0.01 where baselines collapse.
